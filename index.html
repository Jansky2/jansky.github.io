About

My research focuses on understanding ocean dynamics and large-scale circulation processes that regulate climate variability. I am particularly interested in mixed-layer processes, ocean heat fluxes, and their role in driving regional and seasonal sea-level change.

I aim to advance understanding of polar ocean physics, including sea-ice variability and its influence on stratification, circulation, and global climate interactions — combining satellite observations, model simulations, and field data.
